Add your own evidence optional

Import a source array from the local companion. Files stay on this device. Maximum 20 records and 500,000 bytes; pasted JSON is limited to 250,000 characters.

Each source needs a title. Optional: URL, abstract or excerpt, year, and authors. Your text becomes evidence; the tool does not verify pasted claims. Titles may contain up to 1,000 characters, URLs 2,000, and excerpts 20,000.

Review evidence and Local AI context

Remove unwanted records or choose up to six for Local AI. You can undo the last removal until the evidence or selection changes. Without a selection, Local AI uses the first six records, title text up to 200 characters, and the first 800 characters of each excerpt. Full records remain in your notebook. Crossref results are appended after pasted records and fill the default first-six selection only. To choose retrieved records individually, import their JSON before starting; the method demo does not interpret evidence.

Research mode

Downloads a model to your browser (about 1 GB). Requires WebGPU and sufficient device memory. No API key or paid service.

When retrieval is enabled, search terms—or your research question if terms are blank—are sent to Crossref. Records may lack abstracts; this is not a full-text review. Notes and AI reasoning stay on your device.
