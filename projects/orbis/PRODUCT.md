# Orbis

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Infrastructure operations and business economics in a browser workspace. Executive, capacity, and vendor views share one validated analytical scope.

## Users
Existing product audiences: infrastructure and engineering leaders, capacity planners, SREs, FinOps, procurement and business operations analysts.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Keep all three analytical tabs, scope filters, accessible chart inspectors/tables, scenarios, forecasts, investigations, local profiles, saved views, contract registry, imports, exports, sharing and optional companion connection. Demo data remains explicitly synthetic. Browser storage and companion protocols retain existing keys and identifiers. No paid services, runtime dependencies or remote font requests.

## Brand Commitments
User requested a new name for Ledgerline and coordinated new designs for both this app and Napolean Cyber Command. Orbis was proposed with the palette choice the user selected: warm ivory, graphite and cobalt. The user clarified that perfect circles are inspiration for proportions and spacing, not literal circular logos. Orbis keeps its name; the companion security product is now Veyra.

## Evidence on Hand
The existing runnable source and deterministic synthetic infrastructure data. The redesign makes no new customer, security, performance or commercial claims.

## Product Principles
- Preserve truthful units, provenance, limitations and missing-data states.
- Keep analytical actions visible and keyboard accessible.
- Keep user data and saved views compatible across the rebrand.
- Treat the new design as a coordinated product identity, with the current static architecture.
