A recoverable training pipeline

  1. Split the public dataset deterministically into training, validation and test partitions.
  2. Synchronize gradients between CPU Gloo worker processes.
  3. Atomically checkpoint the current model, optimizer, epoch and selected validation model.
  4. Interrupt one worker and restore the group from its last complete checkpoint.
  5. Promote only a completed, validated artifact and identify predictions by its digest.
