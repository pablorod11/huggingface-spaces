01 / Decision & method

Investigate the right source first.

One repeatable batch

Billing operations needs to know which charges deserve review. Provisioning needs to know which orders lack delivery evidence. Data engineering needs to distinguish a replayed extract from a second customer charge.

The model compares September base charges with agreed plans only when CRM and provisioning support a reliable expectation. Conflicting records remain visible and are quarantined from uncertain comparisons.

Controlled scenario. Twenty fictional service orders, all due September 1, plus one orphan billed ID. Flat $50 monthly plans, no mid-month changes, proration, credits, taxes, or usage fees.

Two clocks. Service cutoff: September 30, 23:59:59 UTC. Evidence received through October 3, 00:00:00 UTC is included. Later evidence cannot silently change this run.

04 / Data contract
Define the grain before the join.
Full dictionary ↗
Orders / CRM
One intended snapshot per service_id. Controls agreed monthly plan and commercial status; cannot prove service delivery.
Activation extract
One intended snapshot per service_id. Controls observed provisioning state at cutoff. This fixture is a snapshot, not an event history.
Invoice lines
One intended record per line_id for the selected period. Multiple distinct line IDs are summed, even when the amounts are identical.
Money & tolerance
USD integer cents. Zero-cent tolerance: a $0.01 difference is flagged. Positive variance means billed exceeds expected; negative means a potential shortfall.
Expected amount
Both sources active → plan. Both inactive, or inactive CRM without activation evidence → zero. Missing active-service evidence, source disagreement, or conflicts → unknown.
Output & limitations
One reconciliation row per service and month; one exception per service, month, and rule. No confirmed adjustments. No production scale, real customer data, or employer assignment is implied.
